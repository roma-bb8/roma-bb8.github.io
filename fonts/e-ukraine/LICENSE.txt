e-Ukraine typeface
==================

"e-Ukraine" is the public typeface of the Ministry and the Committee for
Digital Transformation of Ukraine, released for open use on
https://thedigital.gov.ua/fonts .

Author:  Ministry of Digital Transformation of Ukraine (Мінцифра) /
         the "e-Ukraine" design team
Source:  https://thedigital.gov.ua/fonts
         official archive: e-Ukraine.zip (OTF, v1.000, 2020-05-19)

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The .woff2 files in this directory (Light / Regular / Medium / Bold) were
produced from the official OpenType (.otf) sources with fontTools
`ttLib.woff2 compress` — format conversion only, no glyph or metric changes.
The e-Ukraine Head display cut is not bundled here.
